# What ANANKE has built and studied

**Evidence cutoff: 20 November 2026.** This is a public-safe summary of documented research and engineering milestones; it is not an independent rerun of private sources or an assertion that the completed commercial system works end to end.

## The record, not just the roadmap

| Workstream | Documented achievement | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AION Token Reduction Lab | Local Ollama experiments measured actual token and call counts on eligible repeated tasks | Real, **workload-specific laboratory** measurements |
| Necessity and reuse research | Deterministic resolution, result eligibility and model avoidance studied in controlled workflows | Scoped research and candidate behavior, not universal suppression |
| Computational Capital | Validation, proof, current applicability, source-change handling and qualified reuse developed across component work | Several component/offline results; complete current native model-backed lifecycle **not yet accepted** |
| Home Canonical Runtime | Local governance and persistent-state research, independent authority checks and receipt concepts assembled over multiple stages | Component and historical candidate milestones; unified current acceptance open |
| Cloud/control plane | Local organizational, project, authentication, credential and governance paths developed | Historical local evidence; no generally available hosted product certification |
| Website and customer workflow | Project, provider, request, receipt, usage and knowledge surfaces built and exercised in bounded local tests | Historical UX/flow results; current same-engine customer E2E not accepted |
| Minimum Admissible Intelligence | Local M06 candidate v0.1 passed 47/47 focused/affected offline checks | One configured local tier; not a qualified universal multi-provider router |
| ANANKE × JEV experiments | Real provider experiments evaluated upstream necessity and bounded specialized decisions | Controlled external-provider evidence, not a partnership or production integration |
| Four-arm research | Pre-registered comparison included frontier-call reduction and correct outputs | **Overall economic acceptance FAIL** despite favorable bounded metrics |
| Customer value instrumentation | Receipt and accounting concepts and dashboard fields studied | Real current model-backed customer ROI and usage **not demonstrated** |

## Recorded experimental highlights

| Study | What was recorded | What that does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EXP005F** | 1,000 → 5 actual local Ollama calls; 140,060 → 724 measured tokens | Not a universal 193.45× customer guarantee |
| **EXP025A_R5** | 500 → 20 local calls; 70,650 → 2,836 measured tokens; 0 false reuse reported within scope | Not zero errors for all workloads |
| **EXP025A_R10** | 2,000 baseline requests → 465 model calls across 10 waves; final wave 6/200 | Not an external customer or production result |
| **ANANKE × JEV A3** | 600/1,000 requests resolved before JEV, 400 JEV calls in the governed study | Not that 60% of all compute, energy or price was avoided |
| **JEV B R1** | 413/420 bounded decisions correct, with escalation studied | Not a replacement for general-purpose frontier reasoning |
| **Four-Arm R1** | 101 frontier calls vs 240 direct; utility criteria met across matched tasks | **Frozen overall economic criteria were not met** |

The experiments use **different tasks and baselines**. Their reductions cannot be combined or extrapolated to a live product. See [TRL detail](../research/token-reduction-lab.md), [JEV research](../research/ananke-jev.md) and [methodology](../research/methodology.md).

## What remains specifically unproven

A single, recent customer-scoped, authorized run must demonstrate all applicable governance responsibilities together; justified fresh model execution; independently eligible Computational Capital; distinct-request reuse; change-triggered invalidation; real model-call/token receipts; live Website/BFF-to-Cloud readback; isolation, revocation and recovery.

It must also measure **net** advantage, not just count avoided invocations. Historical local successes do not replace this integrated gate.
