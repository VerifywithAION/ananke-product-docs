## 01. Executive summary — slide 1

**ANANKE is pursuing Inference Necessity Infrastructure:** a governance layer intended to decide whether an application actually needs fresh machine intelligence before choosing a model or computing a new result. It aims to preserve previously validated work as **Computational Capital** only while its current authority, applicability and evidence remain valid, escalate genuine novelty/change to appropriate fresh inference, and make execution and estimated avoidance auditable.

**Core positioning:** Intelligence should be spent only when necessary.

**Current maturity, checked 2026-11-24:** research-backed candidate engineering. Scope-limited real-model and external-provider experiments exist. The latest known ANANKE product authority still reports **no complete accepted current customer E2E**, no production-eligible model-backed Computational Capital lifecycle in one customer journey, and no independently measured live customer ROI. **External beta: NO-GO.** The private engine is not distributed by this repository.

**Intended buyers:** AI-platform engineering teams, operators of recurring AI workflows, infrastructure/FinOps owners and enterprise governance teams. Actual contracted buyers and a commercially validated ideal-customer profile **not publicly established**.

## 02. Problem — slide 2

AI applications and agents can repeat previously satisfied work. Existing routing, caching, prompt optimization and smaller-model execution can reduce the price or compute footprint of a call, but **a cheaper call may still be unnecessary**, while naive reuse can be wrong or unauthorized after source, scope or policy changes.

The economic unit ANANKE wants to improve is **total cost of correctly completed obligations**, not raw requests, hits, frontier calls or hypothetical token discounts.

**Critical falsifier:** if model reuse eligibility is rare or the governance/validation overhead outweighs genuinely avoided work, ANANKE may provide little or negative economic benefit.

ANANKE's proposed market is the intersection of enterprise AI infrastructure, agent execution governance, model-consumption management and workflows with measurable task recurrence and source change.

## 04. Product and workflow — slides 4–5

A public conceptual request journey:

1. The customer's existing application or agent presents an authorized obligation.
2. Necessity/current applicability is evaluated before deciding whether to spend fresh intelligence.
3. A supported deterministic result or validated previously completed work may satisfy a request **only if current scope, provenance, evidence and policy permit**.
4. Novel, changed, ambiguous or insufficiently supported work is eligible for a sufficient, authorized fresh executor, or is denied/escalated when required.
5. Decision evidence and actual usage should be recorded, separately identifying counterfactual estimates.
6. Where evidence and eligibility support it, completed work may become **Computational Capital** for future requests; applicable source changes must invalidate affected work.

**Important:** this is the **product contract thesis**, not a certified description of a presently deployed full customer system. See [public conceptual diagram](assets/ananke-decision-map.svg), [product overview](atlas/PRODUCT_OVERVIEW.md), [current maturity matrix](atlas/CAPABILITY_STATUS.md).

**Computational Capital** is *not* a stock of generic cached text, accounting capital on a balance sheet, a learned foundation-model weight or unrestricted entitlement across customers.
