# Minimum Admissible Intelligence

When fresh work is truly necessary, the next question is **what is the least expensive intelligence that can safely satisfy it?** This is distinct from routing every request to the nominally cheapest model.

## The intended hierarchy

1. **No new inference:** deterministic execution, valid proof or eligible existing Capital.
2. **Specialized bounded decisions:** a structured decision model where the task and contract are narrow enough.
3. **Small/local language models:** when configured, qualified and sufficient.
4. **Larger/frontier generative models:** for obligations that cannot safely be completed at earlier levels.
5. **Governed escalation:** human or external verification when required or when no admissible executor exists.

Not all stages are currently integrated or executable in the ANANKE candidate. The hierarchy is a **selection doctrine**; actual selection requires an available, admitted executor and sufficient evidence about its capabilities.

## What the local v0.1 evidence means

As of 2026-11-16, the candidate's versioned M06 local policy had **47/47 relevant offline regressions passing**. It governs permitted local Ollama dispatch with necessity state, scope, budget, provider status and kill-switch conditions, and uses the existing decision/receipt path. In the qualification configuration, **Ollama was the only eligible configured execution tier**.

That proves a local admission/selection boundary under test. It **does not** prove economic optimization across JEV, Ollama and frontier providers, nor end-to-end native acceptance of the full engine.

## Why JEV is interesting

JEV, developed independently by [TypeSafe AI](https://github.com/typesafe-ai), offers a route to structured, bounded decision intelligence. ANANKE and JEV were compared in controlled benchmarks to test a complementary hypothesis:

- **ANANKE** reduces how often fresh decisions need to be made.
- **JEV** can make some fresh bounded decisions at lower provider cost.
- **Frontier models** can handle obligations outside the bounded decision tier.

ANANKE is **not** affiliated with or endorsed by TypeSafe AI, and the comparison is not a claim that JEV replaces a frontier model for general workloads. See [ANANKE × JEV research](../research/ananke-jev.md).

## What must be measured

An admissible selection requires correctness, sufficiency, policy, provider availability, project scope, cost accounting and a refusal/escalation path. A policy that merely chooses the only installed model is not proof of *global* minimum cost.
