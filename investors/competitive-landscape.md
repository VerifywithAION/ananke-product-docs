# ANANKE | Competitive positioning and differentiation

**Revised 24 November 2026. Public product/investor-facing overview; detailed evidence in the linked reports.**

> **The honest competitive thesis:** ANANKE is investigating a governance boundary before new model execution: **is this obligation already safely satisfied by work that remains valid and authorized, or is fresh intelligence genuinely needed?** An attractive premise, but **not** a proven exclusive invention, market moat, or superior production benchmark.

## Strongest pitch line, subject to proof

**ANANKE explores whether previously completed intelligence is still permitted and sufficient to resolve the *current* obligation before spending on a new model execution.** Unlike a slogan about caching, this wording identifies the customer outcome to test: **correct work with minimal justified recomputation under changes and access constraints**.

**Do not say:** “No one else can do it”; “ANANKE invented validated reuse”; “world's first”; “the only system that gates inference”; or “guaranteed 99% savings”.

## What is demonstrated versus still open?

**Measured laboratory research:** local Ollama repeat workloads and ANANKE × JEV bounded studies, with explicit baselines and conditions. These do not prove competitive superiority.

**Adverse finding:** the frozen four-arm evaluation reduced some frontier calls but **failed overall preregistered economics**; a VC should not treat lower frontier utilization as successful net margin.

**Current product status:** unified current customer E2E, model-backed qualified Capital, correct cross-request invalidation under live scope and net customer ROI **remain NOT PROVEN** as of 2026-11-24. External beta remains **NO-GO**.

**Competitive proof still missing:** a full customer-qualified workflow benchmark against **properly configured** competing caching and workflow alternatives, on **the same tasks**, with false reuse, quality, actual tokens/calls and all governance overhead measured.

## Product and IP discipline

ANANKE is not simply a router, cache or new foundation model. Nevertheless, provider routing, task awareness, proof/provenance and selective invalidation have substantial prior art. The question for customers is whether ANANKE can deliver a useful combined control surface. The question for patent counsel is different: the effective filing dates, individual legal claims and freedom to operate in relevant jurisdictions.

Do **not** publish private technical claim charts, internal authority algorithms, experimental sealed fixtures or security details in this public repo. A high-level differentiation brief and a confidential counsel-led FTO review serve different audiences.
