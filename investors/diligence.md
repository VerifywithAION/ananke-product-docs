This is a candid diligence guide for technically sophisticated investors, CIOs, CISOs and AI-infrastructure teams.

## Product and scientific validity

**Is there a product or only a proposal?** There are substantial measured research and scoped engineering milestones, as documented in the [evidence room](evidence.md). The complete current native customer-facing M01–M08 product has **not passed integrated acceptance**, and no general availability is claimed.

**Do the 193.45× and 24.91× results apply to customers?** No. They describe **specific controlled local Ollama workloads**, not arbitrary enterprise workloads, live SaaS, or a customer guarantee.

**Did the four-arm economics succeed?** No. The overall frozen preregistered economic co-primary criterion **failed**, despite favorable frontier-call and correctness results.

**Are results independently reproducible from this repository?** No. Public summaries omit private datasets, internal engine artifacts and raw evaluation materials. Independent reproduction is a future research/disclosure milestone.

**Is ANANKE a semantic cache?** Not as a product definition. Its proposed additional requirement is justified necessity and current validity under identity, source, policy, change and evidence constraints. That difference must be proved under representative tests.

**Why not always use a smaller model?** Small models still compute. Some obligations require stronger reasoning; some are deterministically resolvable. The intended policy must preserve sufficient capability and authorized escalation.

**Will novelty be suppressed?** It must not be. Novel, changing, ambiguous or unsupported obligations require appropriate fresh execution or safe refusal. False suppression is an explicit negative-control target.

## Technology and security

**Where is the source code?** The private ANANKE engine is **not published in this repository**, which offers explanatory documentation, not a runnable SDK or deployment blueprint.

**Has the unified product passed full acceptance?** Not yet. On 20 November 2026, the latest reported customer-authority and proof path remains unexecuted end to end. Existing offline and historical local results are not a substitute.

**Are BYOK and multi-tenant security independently certified?** No public certification is claimed. Customer controls and private mechanisms remain in testing and integration.

**Are all providers supported?** No. The provider studies and local M06 candidate qualify specific experimental scopes only. A public matrix of certified production integrations does not yet exist.

**Are credentials or private internals exposed?** Not in this repository by design. Please do not submit credentials or security details in public GitHub issues.

**Does the system save every response for reuse?** That would be unsafe. The concept requires bounded eligibility and validation. Privacy and rights constraints can prevent retention or reuse.

## Unit economics and market

**What is the business model?** Subscription, project governance and enterprise deployment/support are hypotheses under evaluation; no published final pricing.

**Where is audited traction?** No audited customer revenue, ARR, paid customer count or retention is claimed here.

**What is the market size?** No independently supported TAM/SAM/SOM figure is asserted in these documents.

**Are 90–99% savings guaranteed?** No. Repetition, drift, error constraints, baseline pricing and governance overhead determine each workload's outcome.

**Are energy or emissions reductions measured?** No general physical-energy or carbon savings have been established.

**Why wouldn't a large provider build this?** It could. Strong differentiation must be measured in source-aware correctness, integration, governance, capital validity and total economics—not exclusivity by assertion.

## Financing, IP and release

**Is ANANKE open source?** Its **documentation is publicly visible**. The private engine is not released or licensed here. The rights notice should be reviewed by legal counsel before broader reuse permissions are offered.

**Are there patents?** This repository asserts neither granted patent rights nor freedom-to-operate clearance.

**Is there a live beta?** No external beta is authorized in the current evidence record.

**When does it launch?** Roadmap milestones are conditional and do not carry promised ship dates.

**What proof would materially de-risk it?** One accepted authorized end-to-end customer path with real model-backed Capital, scope/drift safety, actual token accounting and net economics; then representative independent pilots and operational acceptance.

**What can an investor inspect today?** The public research summaries, explicit failure records, current status, competitive context, use cases and roadmap. Private diligence artifacts require separate agreement and rights review.

## Current diligence conclusion

**Investable thesis to investigate:** whether evidence-backed inference necessity can become an economic control point for repetitive AI workflows. **Open proof burden:** reliable integrated product, genuine net customer value, security, distribution and willingness to pay.
