# ANANKE | Investor one-pager

## The company thesis

**ANANKE is building toward Inference Necessity Infrastructure.** The goal is to reduce unjustified recomputation in AI applications by determining when fresh intelligence is required, when independently validated work remains applicable, and how governed execution should be accounted for.

**Tagline:** Intelligence should be spent only when it is necessary.

## Problem

Enterprise agents and AI applications may repeatedly invoke models on obligations already resolved under unchanged conditions. Gateways can route providers, caches can retrieve matches, and prompt tools can lower per-call cost. **None of those alone proves a fresh call is necessary or an older result is safe to reuse.**

## Product

ANANKE's intended value is a governed decision before inference, with four product pillars:

- **Necessity:** assess whether new model work is justified.
- **Computational Capital:** preserve validated prior computation only within continuing eligibility.
- **Safety and change:** escalate novelty and invalidate affected results.
- **Accountability:** distinguish actual usage and justified reuse from modeled counterfactual savings.

The full customer product is still being integrated. This is a **research-backed product thesis**, not a generally available service.

## Buyers and first use cases

Hypothesized buyers: AI-platform teams, enterprise operations leaders and FinOps/governance owners. Initial candidates: policy-backed support classification, versioned document reviews and repeated coding-agent analysis. Select pilots where task correctness, source changes, repeated eligibility and total cost can be measured.

## Commercial thesis

Potential subscription, governed-operation and enterprise support offerings; no published validated pricing, customer revenue, ARR, valuation, contracts or market-size estimate. Economics must include provider spend **and** ANANKE governance overhead.

## Current stage and next milestone

**Research + component/product engineering. Beta NO-GO.** As of 20 November 2026, the complete current customer identity-to-Cloud/receipt path and real model-backed Computational Capital with net customer economics remain **unaccepted**. The next milestone is legitimate authorized end-to-end execution with actual provider tokens, safe reused work, invalidation, and matched net cost/quality.

## Why it may matter

If safely validated reuse scales with repeated enterprise work, the economic unit might become **cost per correct new obligation**, rather than cost per raw request. That potential advantage must be earned through real deployments and evidence.
