# ANANKE | Pitch scripts

These scripts are for an early-stage research-backed product thesis. They deliberately do not assert revenue, customers, a public beta or universal ROI.

## One sentence

**ANANKE is building the infrastructure to decide when AI needs to think again—and when validated work can safely be reused instead.**

## 30-second introduction

"Most AI infrastructure tries to make every model call faster or cheaper. ANANKE asks a more fundamental question: does this task need a new call at all? We are building toward a governance layer that can preserve validated computation as Computational Capital, detect when previous work no longer applies, and use fresh models only when justified. Our laboratory studies show measurable reductions on specific repeated workloads; we are now working to prove that value in one complete, secure customer experience."

## 90-second investor conversation

"AI applications and agents can repeat the same obligation many times, even when a defensible answer already exists. Most platforms optimize routing, caching or token cost after deciding to invoke a model. ANANKE starts one step earlier: should new intelligence be spent?

"The proposed product evaluates whether deterministic work or independently validated prior computation can satisfy the current task under the right source, scope, authorization and freshness conditions. When new or changed work really requires reasoning, it should admit an appropriate, authorized model. Each decision should produce evidence and separate measured calls and tokens from estimated savings.

"The initial research is promising but scoped. In one local Ollama workload, recorded model calls went from 1,000 to five; another went from 500 to twenty. In ANANKE × JEV A3, 600 of 1,000 defined obligations were resolved upstream before specialized model execution. We also disclosed a four-arm economic test that **failed** its overall preregistered threshold. These are not universal production economics.

"Our near-term work is deliberately narrow: finish integrated customer acceptance, demonstrate real model-backed Computational Capital and source-change invalidation, and measure the net cost per correct obligation. We want to validate repeatable enterprise workflows before claiming commercial scale."

## Three-minute follow-on: common questions

**How is this different from a cache?** "A text match does not by itself prove that a result remains correct, current, and permitted. The testable differentiation is evidence-backed reuse eligibility and deliberate escalation when it is not."

**What is already built?** "There is model-backed research, provider comparison evidence, component governance work, local control-plane and customer-interface progress, and focused policy qualifications. The current complete integrated customer product has not yet passed final acceptance."

**Who would pay?** "The strongest early candidates are infrastructure and operations teams with repeated, high-volume, versioned tasks and measurable quality. Subscription and enterprise governance packaging are hypotheses, not announced pricing."

**What's the risk?** "If the workload is too novel or governance costs more than it saves, value may be zero or negative. False reuse is a correctness risk. Our economic and security gates must catch those conditions."

**What's the next proof point?** "A real authorized customer journey with actual inference, valid and invalidated Computational Capital, durable receipts, and honest matched net economics."

## Language to avoid

Do not say: "We cut every AI bill by 99%"; "our platform is ready for customers"; "we invented caching"; "we have achieved 100,000× savings"; "our experiments prove positive net economics"; "we are partnered with JEV/TypeSafe"; or "the product is open source."
