# Related work, positioning and responsible novelty claims

ANANKE belongs to an established ecosystem. Its individual ingredients are not universally new, and no public documentation should claim otherwise.

**Expanded public evidence as of 2026-11-24:** [Prior-art landscape with verified public patents](prior-art-public-landscape.md) and [competitive proof matrix](../investors/competitive-proof-matrix.md). These are high-level, disclosure-limited summaries; private patent claim charts and FTO strategy are excluded.

## Relevant neighboring approaches

| Area | Typical contribution | ANANKE's research question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact / semantic caches | Retrieve previously produced outputs | Is the result **authorized and still applicable** to suppress computation? |
| Retrieval-augmented generation and agent memory | Provide context or evidence to a model | Can validated computation satisfy the obligation **without** a new model call? |
| Prompt compression | Reduce tokens within a call | Is the call necessary at all? |
| Model routing and cascades | Choose among executors | What is the minimum admissible intelligence **after** necessity? |
| Speculative decoding and runtime acceleration | Make generation faster | Can the system avoid or limit fresh generation in the first place? |
| Rule engines and deterministic tools | Solve structured tasks without language models | Which governed deterministic results are sufficient and auditable? |
| Provenance, dependency tracking and invalidation | Track current validity of derived facts | Can past intelligence retain **bounded future applicability**? |
| Case-based reasoning | Adapt prior cases to new problems | Under which independently validated conditions may reuse replace fresh inference? |

This is a **positioning map**, not a legal novelty or freedom-to-operate opinion. The project's internal prior-art review found substantial overlap across caching, normalization, model routing, validation, proof, provenance and task-level reuse. It did **not** establish legal patentability or a right to practice any particular technique.

## The meaningful differentiator to test

The strongest ANANKE research direction is a *governed authority lifecycle*: independent validation, source/proof lineage, prospective applicability, necessity decisions, selectively updated Capital, and recorded execution economics. The key claim to test is **not** that none of these concepts existed before, but whether the complete lifecycle can preserve utility and source authority while materially reducing net fresh inference on real workflows.
