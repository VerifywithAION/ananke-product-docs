## Editorial selection rules

1. Publish educational concepts, user benefits, high-level security guarantees as **design intentions**, and numerically scoped research outcomes.
2. Clearly separate measured, offline, calculated and unproven states.
3. Include failed preregistered scientific outcomes, not only favorable statistics.
4. Remove implementation details and restricted intellectual property, including internal artifact locations and source-verifier internals.
5. Treat publicly discussed product behavior as a roadmap until supported by end-to-end customer evidence.

## Independent reproducibility

The evidence summaries are derived from internal records and selected owner-reported test outputs. They are **not an independently public-audited dataset**, and third parties cannot reproduce the precise experiments from this repository alone. A future rights-cleared reproducibility pack would be a separate release subject to security and IP review.
